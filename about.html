about myself

Business Systems Analyst with experience bridging business needs and technology solutions.

I’m a Business Systems Analyst who enjoys turning complex business problems into clear, practical technology solutions.

My work sits at the intersection of business, technology, and data. I work with stakeholders to understand what they need, translate those needs into clear requirements, and collaborate with technical teams to turn ideas into solutions that work.

I’m particularly interested in the technical side of business analysis. My experience includes requirements analysis, process and data mapping, APIs and integrations, SQL, data analysis, system design, testing, and solution documentation. I also enjoy building hands-on projects using tools such as SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, Postman, MySQL, and web technologies.

How I work

Understand the problem → Ask the right questions → Analyse the data and systems → Define the requirements → Collaborate on the solution → Validate the outcome.
